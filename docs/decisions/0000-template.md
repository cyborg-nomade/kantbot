# ADR 0000: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Name or role
- **Related questions:** [RQ-###](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#research-questions)
- **Related claims:** [K-###](../../CLAIMS.md#register)
- **Supersedes:** None
- **Superseded by:** None

## Context

Describe the problem, why a decision is needed now, and which parts of the
project it constrains. State what remains outside this decision.

## Grounds and claim status

### Textual

Link the controlling primary passages and state only what the cited text
supports. Name relevant A/B differences.

### Interpretive

State the reading adopted or compared, what the text underdetermines, and the
serious alternatives.

### Analogical

Explain the proposed computational analogue and the literal equivalence it
rejects.

### Engineering

Identify constraints introduced for inspectability, testability, usability,
or experimental control rather than attributed to Kant.

## Options considered

### Option A: Name

Describe the option and its relevant advantages and liabilities.

### Option B: Name

Describe the option and its relevant advantages and liabilities.

## Decision

State the chosen option precisely, including any conditions or intentionally
preserved variants.

## Consequences

List the architectural, documentary, and interpretive consequences. Include
costs and limitations as well as benefits.

## Observable consequences

Describe at least one scenario, trace difference, invariant, failure, or test
that should reveal the effect of this decision. Write `None` only when the
record is purely procedural, and explain why a record is still warranted.

## Follow-up

List the claims, specifications, examples, tests, or earlier decisions that
must be updated.
